## What Makes a Password Strong?

- At least 12 characters
- Use uppercase and lowercase letters
- Include numbers
- Include special symbols
- Avoid personal information

---

## Common Password Attacks

1. Brute Force Attack – Attacker tries all possible combinations.
2. Dictionary Attack – Attacker tries common words from a list.

---

## Why Password Length is Important?

Longer passwords take more time to crack and are more secure.

---

## What is Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)?

MFA adds extra security like OTP or fingerprint verification.

---

## Conclusion

Strong and long passwords reduce the risk of hacking and improve security.
